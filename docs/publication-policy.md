# Publication policy

The project may be built and tested locally before publication rights are resolved. TypeSafe benchmark and performance publication terms are account and agreement specific. The current project status is unresolved, so assume that Jev scores, latency, cost, raw outputs, comparative charts, and benchmark statements are private.

Until written permission or a clearly applicable agreement authorizes publication:

- publishable work is limited to harness code, schemas, synthetic fixtures, deterministic baselines, sanitization tools, provenance manifests, and documentation that makes no Jev performance claim;
- live Jev responses and unsanitized traces remain local and ignored;
- reports may be generated privately from cached responses but are not release artifacts;
- release checks must fail when a public artifact contains unresolved Jev outputs or high-risk trace content.

Before a public result release, record the applicable terms, permission scope, date, and restrictions in a private run/release record. If permission is limited, follow those limits exactly. Do not infer permission from other public Jev repositories.

The repository license is intentionally undecided at this stage. Do not add a license file or assert redistribution rights until the owner selects one and upstream dataset terms are checked.
